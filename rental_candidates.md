# Rental candidates — narrative companion

**Trip:** Morgantown, WV · Friday June 12 – Monday June 15, 2026 · 3 BR · good kitchen · three 60-yo friends

> **Source of truth:** `properties.json` is the canonical, machine-readable tracker. This markdown is a snapshot for human discussion as of **2026-05-02**. Structured changes (status updates, new properties, discussion entries) should go into `properties.json`; this document can be regenerated or updated as the conversation evolves. See `tracker_schema.md` for the field semantics and `morgantown_map.html` for the geographic view.

**Stage:** This is still a *representative* survey, not a shortlist. The point is to bracket what each area actually looks like at the rental level, before we narrow toward a specific property or budget.

---

## How to read this

The list is organized into six area buckets that span the realistic options for a long weekend. Each bucket has 2–3 specific listings I could verify exist, plus a one-line read on what the bucket tells us. Properties marked **(type-example)** are descriptions I can confirm from search results but where I haven't pinned the exact URL — useful for "here's the kind of thing this area produces" but the listing IDs need to be re-checked at booking time.

I have *not* tried to compute live nightly totals for our specific dates (June 12–15, 2 nights of weekend pricing + 1 weeknight). The price points below are either listed averages or AirDNA market data; treat them as the order-of-magnitude frame, not a quote.

## Market frame (so we're not flying blind on dollars)

AirDNA's Morgantown short-term-rental data gives a rough distribution of nightly rates for the city as a whole:

| Tier              | Approx. nightly rate |
|-------------------|----------------------|
| Bottom 25%        | ~$85                 |
| Median            | ~$125                |
| Top 25%           | $193+                |
| Top 10%           | $295+                |
| Citywide average  | ~$189                |

For our weekend, Friday and Saturday will both price as weekend nights; Sunday-into-Monday will be a weeknight rate. That tends to push a 3-night spend into roughly 2.4–2.6× the headline nightly rate after fees, in my experience with mid-tier markets — useful only as a back-of-envelope.

## Bucket 1 — In-town walkable (Downtown / South Park / Wiles Hill)

**Vibe:** the postcard option. Walk across the Walnut Street pedestrian bridge to High Street for dinner and back. Most architecturally interesting housing stock in town. Sound: variable — Friday late-night will have student noise downtown, less so in South Park proper.

Representative listings:

- **Beech House — Large 3 BR w/ Deck & Grill** (Airbnb)  
  https://www.airbnb.com/rooms/634034802185517819  
  3 BR, deck, grill — fits the cooking-at-home requirement. Among the more visible whole-house listings in the in-town market.

- **Wilson House** (Airbnb)  
  https://www.airbnb.com/rooms/1089254414490845750  
  Whole house, within ~1 mile of downtown and WVU campus.

- **3 BR / 3 beds / 1 bath rental unit** (Airbnb · ★4.91)  
  https://www.airbnb.com/rooms/916139458128118623  
  Highly-rated 3 BR — the single bath is worth flagging for three adults.

- **The Hidden Gem · Chancery Hill historic district** (Airbnb, type-example)  
  Tucked into Chancery Hill, walking distance to downtown and the WVU downtown campus. The kind of older, story-rich house this area is full of.

**What this bucket tells us:** the in-town market is mostly in older houses, often with quirky bathroom counts (one bath for three bedrooms is common). Prices for whole houses cluster around the city-wide median to top-quartile range; expect the better-equipped historic homes to land in the $150–$220/night zone.

## Bucket 2 — Suncrest / WVU-adjacent (north of downtown)

**Vibe:** postwar suburban. Wider streets, larger lots, newer kitchens. The deepest 3-BR vacation-rental inventory in the area, partly because the neighborhood is built around the WVU football/hospital traffic. Driving for dinner is the trade-off — 5–10 minutes to downtown.

Representative listings:

- **Suncrest Haven · Close to WVU/Hospitals** (Airbnb · ★4.93, 138 reviews)  
  https://www.airbnb.com/rooms/43868312  
  3 BR, sleeps 6–8, hot tub on deck, full kitchen with fireplace, parking for 4–5. ~1 mi to WVU Evansdale and the stadium. Very strong review profile.

- **Enchanting Family House w/ Balcony · <3 Mi to WVU** (VRBO / Evolve)  
  VRBO: https://www.vrbo.com/9482360ha · Evolve: https://evolve.com/vacation-rentals/us/wv/morgantown/441876  
  3 BR / 2 BA · 1,450 sq ft · sleeps 5 · listed average **$137/night**. A useful concrete data point — solidly in the median band.

- **3 BR / 4 beds / 2.5 baths home** (Airbnb · ★4.67)  
  https://www.airbnb.com/rooms/27955982  
  Larger floorplan, 2.5 BA — more comfortable for three men than the single-bath in-town options.

**What this bucket tells us:** Suncrest is where the "newer, more amenities, two-and-a-half baths, hot tub, full kitchen" listings live. Expect $135–$200/night. This is probably the path of least resistance if cooking-at-home and personal space matter more than walking to dinner.

## Bucket 3 — Westover / across the river

**Vibe:** separate municipality. Mostly residential, small-house housing stock from the glass-works and railroad era. Quick to cross the Westover Bridge into downtown but psychologically a different town. Often the value position in the market.

Representative listings:

- **Westover whole-home market** (VRBO area page)  
  https://www.vrbo.com/vacation-rentals/usa/west-va/westover  
  VRBO shows ~172 properties in this slice. The Enchanting Family House (above, $137/night) is technically counted here on some platforms.

- **Westover area Airbnbs** (catalog)  
  https://www.airbnb.com/westover-wv/stays  
  Mix of small whole-home rentals and rooms.

**What this bucket tells us:** Westover tends to undercut in-town walkable by 10–20% for comparable square footage, but the listings are often older houses with quirkier layouts. Worth scanning for value plays; not worth choosing if walking to dinner matters.

## Bucket 4 — Cheat Lake (~10 minutes east)

**Vibe:** lake-house feel. Decks, hot tubs, water views. Gated developments mixed with mid-century cottages. A real change of mood from town — you'd drive in for any meal out, but the property itself becomes the destination.

Representative listings:

- **Luxurious Morgantown Retreat with Stunning Views** (VRBO)  
  https://www.vrbo.com/9888312ha  
  3 BR / 3 BA · 1,800 sq ft · two living rooms · smart TVs · central A/C. The "splurge but still 3 BR" anchor for the lake bucket.

- **Our Lovely Lake House** (VRBO)  
  3 BR / 2 BA, sleeps 9, 10/10 rating, 8 reviews. Sleeps-9 capacity for three of us means generous personal space.  
  (Search via https://www.vrbo.com/vacation-rentals/houses/usa/west-virginia/cheat-lake)

- **The Escape Pod @ Cheat Lake · Private Hot Tub** (VRBO)  
  https://www.vrbo.com/3786507  
  Smaller but heavily amenitized — useful as the "this is what a hot-tub-and-deck Cheat Lake property looks like" reference point.

**What this bucket tells us:** Cheat Lake skews higher than in-town. Expect $180–$300/night for 3 BR with views or water access. Cooking-at-home becomes more central to the experience because it's the natural rhythm of a lake-house weekend.

## Bucket 5 — Cooper's Rock corridor / Bruceton Mills (~20–30 minutes east)

**Vibe:** cabin in the woods. The state-forest hinterland. This is where the trip changes character — full retreat, hiking out the back door, no walking to anything. Best for a group that's leaning into the natural-environs half of the trip plan.

Representative listings:

- **The Homestead** (near Cooper's Rock, type-example)  
  3 BR / 2 BA · sleeps 6 · queen + two doubles. The most directly fitted 3 BR option I found in the corridor.

- **Coopers Rock Retreat** (Airbnb)  
  https://www.airbnb.com/rooms/39004635  
  Listed as an apartment-style rental in Bruceton Mills — re-check the bedroom count; it may read smaller than 3 BR.

- **Mountain Creek Cabins** (private operator, multi-cabin retreat)  
  https://mountaincreekcabins.com/  
  Eight luxury log cabins with hot tubs, fireplaces, full kitchens. **Caveat:** each cabin sleeps up to 4 — for the three of us, one cabin sleeps three but doesn't give us "3 bedrooms" in the rental sense. Worth a phone call; they may have a larger property.

**What this bucket tells us:** the strict "3 BR rental with good kitchen" filter is harder to satisfy in the rural corridor — much of the inventory is 2 BR cabins. Most candidates here will be 2 BR + sleeper sofa or two adjacent cabins. Pricing is wide: from ~$150/night for a small cabin to $300+ for a large lodge.

## Bucket 6 — Star City / Mon River corridor (north)

**Vibe:** small separately-incorporated town just north of Morgantown, on the Mon River Trail. Light vacation-rental inventory; primarily useful as a *recreation anchor* (trail access) rather than a place to stay. Including for completeness so we don't ignore it by accident.

Representative listings:

- **Star City whole-home market** (VRBO area page)  
  https://www.vrbo.com/vacation-rentals/usa/west-va/star-city  
  Sparse — what shows up is mostly listings adjacent to Star City rather than in it.

**What this bucket tells us:** thin inventory. Probably not worth a serious 3 BR search here unless something unusual surfaces; better to treat the trail itself as a daytime destination from one of the other buckets.

## Cross-bucket synthesis (just observations, no scoring yet)

Three structural patterns worth flagging before we start prioritizing:

1. **Single-bathroom 3 BRs are common in the in-town historic stock.** Three adult men sharing one bathroom for three days is fine but worth being explicit about. South Park and Wiles Hill houses tend toward this pattern; Suncrest does not.

2. **The "big kitchen + hot tub on deck" pattern lives in Suncrest and Cheat Lake.** If cooking Saturday and Sunday dinners is central to the weekend, those two buckets dominate. The historic in-town options are charming but the kitchens are hit-or-miss.

3. **The town/retreat axis matters more than the dollars.** The price spread within each bucket is roughly the same as the spread between buckets. The harder question — *is this a "walk to dinner downtown" weekend or a "three guys with a kitchen and a porch" weekend?* — will narrow the search faster than any budget filter will.
